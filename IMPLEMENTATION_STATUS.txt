RISK ASSESSMENT FORMULA
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Risk Score = (
    ML_SCORE(0-1) × 0.35 +         [ Fraud probability from ML model ]
    IP_REP(0-100) × 0.25 +         [ IP threat intelligence score ]
    DEVICE(0-100) × 0.15 +         [ Device trust level ]
    VELOCITY(0-100) × 0.10 +       [ Rate limiting penalty ]
    ACCT_AGE(0-100) × 0.10 +       [ Account age adjustment ]
    BEHAVIOR(0-100) × 0.05         [ Behavioral anomaly penalty ]
  ) × 100

  Decision Tree:
  ├─ 0-20   → ALLOW                (No action)
  ├─ 21-40  → CHALLENGE            (Require SMS)
  ├─ 41-80  → HIGH_RISK            (SMS + extra checks)
  └─ 80+    → CRITICAL             (Block + manual review)
